1. Legal entity, current licence and Norway passport, exact AISP/TSP role,

   restoration, service credits, status feed, escalation, incident/update/root-

   continuity summaries, isolation, encryption/key custody, access review,
   vulnerability response, regions, subprocessors, transfers, retention, backups,
   data-subject assistance and breach handling.
6. API lifecycle: limits, idempotency, pagination, degraded-bank state,
   deprecation/version overlap, replay, gaps, reconnect and consent closure.
7. Exit: notice, export window, consent migration/closure, secret removal,
   deletion/backup certificate, transition assistance, liability, audit/regulator
