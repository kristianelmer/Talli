# Preserve year-specific admission promises

Kristian approved complete 2025 company-year support alongside 2026 in #226. Each year has its own versioned capability manifest, full-year reconstruction dates and accepted company-year promise. Keep the original 2026 manifest and historical acceptances unchanged; a new 2025 manifest adds the approved year without changing what a 2026 owner accepted.

Eligibility context and rechecks must select the requested company-year. A supported 2026 admission cannot authorize a blocked or unadmitted 2025 year. The default remains the latest admitted supported year when no year is requested. Ownership, current agreements, MFA and eligibility remain required; year admission does not open payment, release or live-provider gates.
