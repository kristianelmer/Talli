# Supported ledger patterns

Status: normative implementation input for issue #188

Source cut: 2026-08-27 (Europe/Oslo)

Product boundary: the [owner-approved resolution of issue #172 (2026-08-26)](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104)

## Purpose and interpretation

This document maps the legally relevant facts for Talli's supported small Norwegian holding-company ledger to deterministic product behavior. It is not a statement that other lawful accounting is forbidden. “Hard block” means that Talli must not guess, silently approximate, or mark the affected company-year complete; it must stop the affected workflow and preserve truthful read/export access, as required by the [#172 product decision](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

The statutory sources establish accounting, documentation, tax, and company-law requirements. The journal shapes, source identifiers, idempotency keys, balanced-entry invariant, and deliberately narrower eligibility rules below are Talli controls for meeting those requirements. They are not presented as statutory wording.

## Cross-cutting ledger invariants

### Complete calendar-year reconstruction

- An ordinary accounting year is the calendar year under [regnskapsloven § 1-7](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A71-7), subject to exceptions that Talli does not support in this route. Every fact needed for mandatory specifications and reporting must be booked, and booking must be current by the statutory deadlines, under [bokføringsloven § 7](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A77). Annual statements also show prior-year comparatives under [regnskapsloven § 6-6](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A76-6).
- Talli therefore requires an evidenced opening balance and every in-scope transaction from 1 January through the selected cut-off before it can claim year completeness. This “join at any time and reconstruct from 1 January” rule is a [#172/Talli completeness boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104), not a statutory onboarding rule.
- Opening assets, liabilities, and equity must reproduce the prior closing balances and reconcile to prior annual accounts, tax evidence, bank statements, shareholder/investment records, and loan records. Material balance items require documentation under [bokføringsloven § 11](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A711), and Altinn's official close guidance requires material balance accounts, including bank, to be reconciled to evidence ([Altinn: balance documentation](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/dokumentasjon-av-balansen-avstemminger/)).
- Hard block: a non-calendar year, missing prior close, unexplained opening difference, incomplete bank period, unsupported transaction, or a gap that prevents complete reconstruction. Do not invent a suspense opening balance or mark the year ready; preserve the partial ledger and read/export access under the [#172 fail-closed rule](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

### Documentation, balanced entries, and traceability

- Bookkeeping must be orderly, complete, real, accurate, current, documented, traceable in both directions, readable, retained, and protected against improper alteration or loss under [bokføringsloven § 4](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A74). Each booked fact must be supported by correct and complete documentation that justifies the entry under [bokføringsloven § 10](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A710); Altinn likewise requires vouchers to be complete, business-related, immutable after issuance, and systematically identifiable ([Altinn: voucher requirements](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/krav-til-bilag/)).
- Every Talli journal must balance total debit and credit in NOK, carry company, company-year, event date, semantic pattern/version, source-document IDs, counterparty where relevant, and an idempotency key. Balanced double entry is Talli's deterministic journal invariant for accuracy and audit trail; it is not attributed as verbatim wording of the cited statute. NOK-only processing is the [#172 product boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104). The law generally requires NOK under [bokføringsloven § 8](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A78), while [bokføringsforskriften § 4-2](https://lovdata.no/forskrift/2004-12-01-1558/%C2%A74-2) permits specified foreign-currency accounting; Talli's rejection of foreign-currency cases is intentionally narrower than the law.
- An imported bank row is corroborating evidence, not by itself sufficient classification evidence. Talli may post only when the bank row plus invoice, decision, agreement, statement, assessment, or other source proves the supported pattern and amount. That control implements the reality requirement in [bokføringsloven § 4](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A74) and the documentation requirement in [§ 10](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A710).

### Corrections and duplicate prevention

- After the deadline in [bokføringsloven § 7](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A77), booked information may not be changed or deleted. A correction must be a new documented entry that completely reverses the original; the original and correction remain visible under [bokføringsloven § 9](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A79).
- Talli must use append-only reversal plus a replacement entry, each linked to the original source/event and correction reason. Retrying the same event must return the same result or a no-op, never a second economic posting. The idempotency behavior is a Talli control supporting the protection and traceability principles in [bokføringsloven § 4](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A74).
- A prior-period accounting error may also change annual-statement presentation: [regnskapsloven § 4-3](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A74-3) generally takes prior-year error correction against equity, while small entities may use the good-practice result route; [NRS 8 section 7.3](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) requires errors to be corrected and describes that policy choice. Talli must freeze one permitted policy for the company-year and hard-block an ambiguous prior-year/principle/estimate distinction rather than disguise it as an ordinary current-year reversal.

### Period close and locks

- A booked period must be closed within the statutory update deadlines and then protected against change or deletion; the close functionality must be documented and that documentation retained for five years under [bokføringsforskriften § 7-6](https://lovdata.no/forskrift/2004-12-01-1558/%C2%A77-6). Altinn describes a closed period as documented and corrected, after which booked information may not be changed or deleted ([Altinn: accounting requirements](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/krav-til-regnskapet/)).
- Before locking, Talli must pass source completeness, balanced-journal, duplicate, unsupported-pattern, bank reconciliation, material-balance documentation, and reporting reconciliation checks. After locking, only the linked correction flow may change economic effect. A lock is not proof that a year is complete if an unsupported or unexplained item remains.
- Hard block: any incomplete source trail, unreconciled material balance, unresolved bank row, unsupported transaction, or check failure. The system must never weaken the lock to “fix in place.”

### Periodization and estimates

- Transactions are recognized at transaction value and time, income when earned, costs in the same period as associated income, and unrealized losses under the principles in [regnskapsloven § 4-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A74-1). Altinn's official guidance likewise places income and cost in the period to which they belong ([Altinn: periodization](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/periodisering-tidfesting/)).
- Uncertain amounts require the best estimate based on information available when the annual accounts are prepared under [regnskapsloven § 4-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A74-2). Talli may automate only an estimate whose permitted method and inputs are frozen and deterministic. Otherwise it must hard-block the affected completion step for professional/user resolution; “zero,” “same as cash,” and silent carry-forward are not safe defaults.

## Deterministic supported patterns

The entries below use semantic categories, not hard-coded chart-of-account numbers. Every entry also carries the cross-cutting source, balance, identity, and idempotency fields above.

### Opening balance and current-year rebuild

Required facts are the prior closing trial balance or annual accounts, ownership/investment positions, every loan balance and accrued interest, tax balances, bank statements covering the entire year, and all in-scope transaction documents. This evidence is required by the completeness principle in [bokføringsloven § 4](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A74), current-booking rule in [§ 7](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A77), balance-documentation rule in [§ 11](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A711), and official reconciliation guidance ([Altinn: balance documentation](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/dokumentasjon-av-balansen-avstemminger/)).

Talli posts one balanced opening journal dated 1 January, preserving the semantic prior-close classifications, then posts current-year source events in event-date order. It proves `opening + current-year movements = closing` for every semantic account and `book bank = statement bank` at each reconciliation cut-off. This reconstruction shape is a [Talli control for the #172 complete-year promise](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

Hard block any unresolved opening difference, unavailable evidence, source overlap/duplicate, out-of-period entry, unsupported prior-close classification, or prior accounts whose accounting policy cannot be continued consistently as required by [regnskapsloven § 4-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A74-4).

### Ordinary administration cost

Required facts are a valid supplier invoice or receipt, supplier identity, document and delivery dates, description, amount, payment/bank evidence, and an explicit business purpose. The documentation must justify the posting under [bokføringsloven § 10](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A710); where the document is a sales document, the required fields are specified in [bokføringsforskriften § 5-1-1](https://lovdata.no/forskrift/2004-12-01-1558/%C2%A75-1-1).

For a paid, period-correct ordinary administration invoice, post debit `ordinary administration expense` and credit `bank`. If unpaid at period end, credit `supplier payable`, then debit the payable and credit bank on payment. An expense has tax deduction only to the extent incurred to acquire, maintain, or secure taxable income under [skatteloven § 6-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A76-1). Costs connected to income under the exemption method have the separate rule in [skatteloven § 6-24](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A76-24), which expressly excludes acquisition and realization costs from deduction.

Hard block VAT-bearing workflow, payroll, customer invoicing, inventory/property/operating purchases, private or mixed purpose, missing business purpose, capitalizable expenditure, share acquisition/realization cost, foreign currency, or any ambiguous § 6-1/§ 6-24 classification. These are outside the [#172 ordinary-administration route](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104); the block is a product boundary, not a conclusion that the expense is unlawful.

### Norwegian share and simple listed-fund purchase or sale

This route covers an evidenced purchase or sale of one ordinary class of shares in a Norwegian private company, a simple listed share, or a fund unit for which the instrument, ownership, accounting classification, tax classification, NOK amounts, and year-specific fund data are all deterministic. It implements the [#172 accepted private-share and clear listed/fund boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104); it does not make active trading or complex instruments eligible.

Require the instrument identifier and legal issuer/fund, Norwegian/EØS status used by the tax engine, class/unit identifier, trade and settlement dates, quantity, NOK unit price, gross consideration, separately stated broker/transaction costs, counterparty or broker execution evidence, bank settlement, pre- and post-trade positions, acquisition purpose and accounting classification, and the versioned tax result. A private-company purchase must also have the transfer agreement and evidence that consent and pre-emption restrictions are cleared. Shares are transferable only within statutory, articles, and agreement restrictions under [aksjeloven § 4-15](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-15), and the new owner must be entered after the acquisition is notified and proved under [§ 4-7](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-7). Skatteetaten identifies purchase consideration plus broker-like acquisition costs as input value and requires the acquisition to be notified and entered in the shareholder register for private shares ([Skatteetaten: share purchases](https://www.skatteetaten.no/person/skatt/hjelp-til-riktig-skatt/aksjer-og-verdipapirer/om/fastsette-inngangsverdien/kjop-av-aksjer/)).

Freeze the company's permitted accounting classification and valuation policy before posting:

| Case | Deterministic accounting route |
| --- | --- |
| Subsidiary, associate, or other investment held for long-term ownership under the cost method | Purchase: debit the applicable long-term investment for purchase price plus direct purchase costs and credit bank/settlement payable. Sale: debit bank/settlement receivable for net proceeds, credit the disposed carrying amount, and recognize the residual accounting gain or loss in the applicable investment-income/financial-loss category. [NRS 8 sections 4.3.3, 4.3.3.1, and 4.3.3.2](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) require cost-method investments at acquisition cost, subject to impairment, and separate subsidiary/associate presentation. |
| Simple listed share or clear fund unit classified as a current investment | Purchase: debit current investment for purchase price plus direct purchase costs and credit bank/settlement payable. Talli freezes the small-entity election in [NRS 8 section 4.4.4](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) to lower of cost and fair value and its permitted FIFO simplification for interchangeable shares; each required period-end quote and any write-down/reversal remain separately evidenced. Sale derecognizes the selected carrying amount against net proceeds and records the residual financial gain/loss. A pre-existing different permitted policy must be continued consistently under [regnskapsloven § 4-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A74-4) or the route blocks. |

Maintain accounting carrying value and tax input value as separate versioned ledgers. For shares, taxable gain/loss is consideration less input value under [skatteloven § 10-32](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-32), and shares of the same class are disposed of first-in-first-out under [§ 10-36](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-36). A qualifying corporate share gain is generally exempt and the corresponding loss non-deductible under [§ 2-38](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A72-38); Talli still records the accounting gain/loss and passes the separate exemption adjustment to tax. Skatteetaten's company guidance confirms the ordinary exemption-method consequence for company share sales ([Skatteetaten: company shares](https://www.skatteetaten.no/bedrift-og-organisasjon/skatt/skattemelding-naringsdrivende/fradrag/aksjer/aksjer-i-naring-selskapets-aksjer/)).

For a fund unit, the tax engine must receive the provider-reported fund type and equity shares for the acquisition and sale years. [Skatteloven § 10-20 second, third, sixth, and ninth paragraphs](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-20) splits distributions between dividend and interest treatment, proportionally limits exemption-method treatment of a sale using the average equity share in acquisition and sale years, and specifies consequences when required fund data is not reported. Talli's supported route requires complete data and does not silently substitute the statutory missing-data outcome for an eligibility fact.

Hard block foreign/private issuer ambiguity, foreign currency, multiple or unusual classes/rights, derivatives/crypto/structured or leveraged products, active trading, short sales, margin, securities lending, options, an investment account wrapper, in-kind or non-cash settlement, contingent/deferred consideration, related-party price ambiguity, missing lot or fund-equity data, an unresolved exemption-method result, an impairment indicator requiring judgment, or a sale whose quantity exceeds the proved position. The presence of an impairment indicator under [NRS 8 section 4.3.3](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) stops the deterministic long-term route until an authorized, evidenced assessment is supplied.

### Simple ownership change and position continuity

Every supported investment purchase/sale, cash issue, registered nominal capital change, or direct owner-to-owner sale must create one immutable ownership event. An event records issuer, owner, class, effective/legal date, old and new share/unit count, nominal value where applicable, consideration event, shareholder-register or securities-depository reference, and resulting ownership/vote percentages. Investment and capital events link to the corresponding economic journal; a direct sale between two owners of the Talli company explicitly records `no company journal` and the reason. The shareholder register must identify each owner and their number, numbers, and class of shares under [aksjeloven § 4-5](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-5); a transferee generally exercises shareholder rights only after entry or proved notification without an applicable transfer restriction under [§ 4-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-2).

For a direct owner-to-owner cash sale, require the signed transfer agreement, seller and buyer identity/residence, exact one-class shares and price, notification to the board, resolved consent/pre-emption conditions, dated shareholder-register entry, and resulting ownership/votes. The acquirer must notify the company without delay under [aksjeloven § 4-12](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-12), and the company must enter the proved, unblocked new owner without delay and preserve former-owner data for ten years under [§ 4-7](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-7). Because the company is neither buyer nor seller and receives/pays no consideration, this event updates the cap table/shareholder evidence only: it must not post company bank, investment, share-capital, share-premium, retained-earnings, gain, or loss lines. Brønnøysundregistrene confirms that owner changes are maintained in the shareholder register rather than registered as owner changes with the agency ([official shareholder guidance](https://www.brreg.no/aksjeselskap/aksjeeier/)).

Keep the buyer's purchase-price/input-value lot separate from the company's historical paid-in-capital attribute for the transferred shares. Purchase price plus qualifying acquisition costs determines share input value under [skatteloven § 10-32](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-32), while paid-in capital remains attached to the individual share independently of who paid it according to [Skatte-ABC A-6-7.7](https://www.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/a-6-aksjer--utbytte/A-6.060/A-6.077/). An owner-to-owner sale changes the owner and buyer lot; it must not reset or replace the share's paid-in-capital history.

A registry/VPS update that merely confirms an already posted transfer changes event status and position evidence; it does not create a second economic journal. A correction appends a reversing position event linked to the original rather than rewriting history, applying the traceability and protection principles in [bokføringsloven § 4](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A74). At every event Talli proves `opening units + acquired/issued units − disposed/cancelled units = closing units` for each owner/instrument/class and reconciles that result to the shareholder register or securities-depository statement.

The event must flow to the annual shareholder-register report, which all Norwegian AS generally file by 31 January and, from June 2026, submit through an end-user system under [Skatteetaten's current filing guidance](https://www.skatteetaten.no/bedrift-og-organisasjon/rapportering-og-bransjer/aksjonarregisteroppgaven/). If the event creates or changes a registrable beneficial owner, Talli must require the separate beneficial-owner update within 14 days under [Brønnøysundregistrene's current guidance](https://www.brreg.no/reelle-rettighetshavere/). Filing/registry status is tracked separately from the economic and shareholder-register event; neither creates a duplicate journal.

Run the [#172 quiet eligibility recheck](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104) before finalizing any change that affects control, group status, consolidation, tax-group thresholds, shareholder residence, class rights, or filing facts. Hard block a gift/inheritance/gift-sale, matrimonial or estate transfer, merger/demerger/conversion, beneficial-owner or nominee mismatch, disputed/conditional transfer, unresolved consent or pre-emption right, own-share transaction, split/consolidation/bonus issue, missing effective date, or any change that makes consolidation required or uncertain under [Altinn's official group-accounts guidance](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/arsregnskap/arsregnskap-for-konsern/).

### Ordinary cash capital increase

Support only a NOK cash subscription in the Talli company, with one ordinary share class, the statutory separate contribution account, no debt conversion or in-kind contribution, and complete corporate and registration evidence. Require the board proposal or registered board authority, general-meeting/board decision and amended articles, exact nominal increase, issue price, subscribers and allocations, subscription and payment deadlines, dividend-right date, signed subscriptions, restricted-account receipt, independent confirmation of the cash contribution, Foretaksregisteret submission and registration, updated articles, and updated shareholder register. [Aksjeloven § 10-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A710-1) assigns the decision and its minimum terms; [§ 10-9](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A710-9) requires timely registration, full contribution before filing, and permitted confirmation; [§ 10-10](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A710-10) makes the registered amount the legal increase; and [§ 10-13](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A710-13) requires cash on a separate account that the company cannot use before registration unless the general meeting uses its stated exception. Brønnøysundregistrene lists the decision, articles, payment confirmation, three-month filing route, and shareholder-register update ([official capital-increase procedure](https://www.brreg.no/aksjeselskap/aksjekapital/forhoyelse-av-aksjekapital/)); a bank statement alone is not the required confirmation ([official contribution-confirmation requirements](https://www.brreg.no/aksjeselskap/aksjekapital/bekreftelse-av-mottatt-aksjeinnskudd/)).

Apply the staged accounting in [NRS 8 sections 5.1 and 5.1.1.1](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf):

1. Once a qualifying decision and binding subscription exist by the balance date, credit `unregistered capital increase`; debit `subscription receivable` until paid, then debit `restricted contribution bank` and credit the receivable. If full timely payment is uncertain, do not recognize the increase and hard-block close.
2. At Foretaksregisteret registration, debit `unregistered capital increase`, credit registered share capital for the nominal increase, and credit share premium for issue price above nominal. Reclassify the released restricted contribution account to ordinary bank. Registered share capital must always equal the register.
3. Post direct documented issue costs and their supported tax effect against share premium/paid-in capital, not ordinary administration expense, as required by [NRS 8 sections 5.1 and 5.1.2](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf).

Talli pins unregistered capital increase to `2030`, registered share capital to `2000`, and share premium to `2020`, matching the [Skatteetaten-published SAF-T standard-account list](https://github.com/Skatteetaten/saf-t/blob/master/General%20Ledger%20Standard%20Accounts/CSV/General_Ledger_Standard_Accounts_4_character_2020.csv). Its restricted cash-contribution subaccount is `1921`, mapped with ordinary bank deposits (`1920`) for SAF-T grouping; `1950` remains exclusively the tax-withholding bank account and must never carry share contributions.

The ownership event uses the rights date in the valid decision, subject to the rule that general shareholder rights arise no later than registration under [aksjeloven § 10-11](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A710-11), and then recalculates shareholder counts, percentages, and votes. Hard block non-cash contribution, debt conversion/set-off, fund/bonus issue, underwriting or conditional subscription, partial/late/unconfirmed payment, the § 10-13 direct-use exception, expired/refused filing, multiple classes, special rights, foreign subscriber, employee/options program, issue-cost or tax ambiguity, invalid authority/pre-emption facts, or a register mismatch. A decision or bank deposit alone must never be represented as registered share capital.

For every issued share, the capital event also records nominal amount, paid premium, issue price/input value, subscriber, acquisition date, and paid-in-capital tax attribute. These per-share attributes must reconcile to the total nominal capital and premium journals and remain distinct for later ownership transfers; [skatteloven § 10-32](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-32) governs purchase/input value, while [Skatte-ABC A-6-7.7](https://www.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/a-6-aksjer--utbytte/A-6.060/A-6.077/) confirms that paid-in capital attaches to each share rather than the identity of the original payer.

### Registered capital reduction solely for loss coverage

Support only a proportional reduction of nominal value across the single ordinary class, with unchanged owners and share count, used entirely to cover an evidenced loss that cannot be covered otherwise. No cash, asset, receivable, or other value may pass to an owner. Require the board proposal and reasons, general-meeting decision and amended articles, latest approved annual accounts or valid interim balance, exact uncovered loss and proof that other equity has first been used, old/new nominal value and share capital, timely Foretaksregisteret submission and registration, updated articles/shareholder register, and the resulting dividend restriction. [Aksjeloven § 12-1 first paragraph no. 1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-1) permits only loss that cannot be covered otherwise; [§ 12-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-2) identifies the governing balance; [§ 12-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-4) requires filing within two months; and [§ 12-5](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-5) makes the loss-coverage reduction effective on registration without creditor notice and restricts dividends for three years unless its stated exception is met. Brønnøysundregistrene confirms the one-step no-creditor-notice loss route and required documents ([official capital-reduction procedure](https://www.brreg.no/aksjeselskap/aksjekapital/nedsettelse-av-aksjekapital/)).

Apply [NRS 8 sections 5.1.1.5 and 5.5](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf): if the valid decision exists by the balance date but is not yet registered, debit `unregistered capital reduction` as a negative paid-in-equity line and credit uncovered loss; on registration, debit registered share capital and credit `unregistered capital reduction`. If already registered when first posted, debit registered share capital and credit uncovered loss directly. The entry changes equity classification, not total equity or cash. Registered share capital must match Foretaksregisteret and remain at least NOK 30,000 under [aksjeloven § 3-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A73-1). Talli records the three-year dividend block from the registration date and makes the owner-dividend route fail closed until a documented § 12-5 exception applies.

The account and SAF-T mapping pins unregistered capital reduction to `2033`, registered share capital to `2000`, and uncovered loss to `2080`, using the same [Skatteetaten-published standard-account list](https://github.com/Skatteetaten/saf-t/blob/master/General%20Ledger%20Standard%20Accounts/CSV/General_Ledger_Standard_Accounts_4_character_2020.csv).

The loss-coverage reduction does not erase the historical tax position for paid-in capital on each surviving share. Skatteetaten's current Skatte-ABC states that paid-in capital written down for loss can later be recovered and remains attached to each share after the write-down ([A-6-7.7](https://www.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/a-6-aksjer--utbytte/A-6.060/A-6.077/)). Talli must therefore preserve the per-share paid-in-capital history unchanged even though every repayment workflow remains blocked.

Hard block cancellation/redemption of shares, unequal nominal reduction, repayment or other distribution of paid-in capital, transfer to a free fund, simultaneous increase/reduction, creditor-notice route, missing/unapproved loss evidence, remaining cover in other equity, interim-balance ambiguity, below-minimum capital, an expired/refused decision or one not registered within the § 12-4 deadline, owner/class/share-count change, or any cash movement. Repayment is a different lawful company-law route under [aksjeloven § 12-1 first paragraph no. 2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-1) and the creditor procedure in [§ 12-6](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-6), and its tax treatment depends on shareholder-specific paid-in-capital and input-value tracking under [skatteloven § 10-11 second paragraph](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-11) and [§ 10-35](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-35). It remains expressly outside this deterministic route even when legally permissible.

### Bank interest and company tax

For interest income, require a bank statement/advice identifying principal account, period, gross interest, withholding or fee if any, and booking/value date. Post debit `bank or interest receivable` and credit `bank interest income`; settle a receivable against bank when paid. For interest expense, use the loan route below. Interest is capital income under [skatteloven § 5-20](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A75-20), and the annual result layout separately identifies group interest and other financial income/cost under [regnskapsloven § 6-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A76-1).

For company tax, the ledger consumes the versioned result of the supported tax calculation; it does not infer tax from a bank debit. Post current tax expense against `tax payable`, change in deferred tax against `deferred tax liability/asset`, and payments/refunds against the corresponding tax balance. [NRS 8 sections 6.1.1.2 and 7.2.6](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) define tax expense as current tax plus change in deferred tax, require small taxable entities to account for deferred tax (while allowing a limited omission of deferred-tax assets), and classify current tax as short-term debt; [regnskapsloven § 6-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A76-1) presents tax expense after result before tax.

Hard block interest that cannot be separated from principal/fees, non-bank or foreign-source interest, foreign withholding/tax credit, indexed/derivative/convertible instruments, related-party interest whose tax treatment is unresolved, or a tax amount not produced by the applicable supported tax engine. Debt interest is generally deductible under [skatteloven § 6-40](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A76-40), but [skatteloven § 6-41](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A76-41) can limit deductions in group and related-party cases; Talli must never turn those provisions into an unconditional “all interest deductible” rule.

### Ordinary NOK bank loan

Require the signed lender agreement, Norwegian company borrower, NOK principal, disbursement date, term, interest basis, repayment schedule, fees, lender statements, and bank matches. Post disbursement as debit `bank` / credit `bank-loan principal`; each lender-confirmed payment debits principal plus separately documented interest/fee categories and credits bank. Present debt by contractual maturity and separately from related-party debt; [NRS 8 section 6.2.1.3](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) addresses short-term debt to credit institutions, while the annual layout distinguishes financial cost under [regnskapsloven § 6-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A76-1).

Hard block foreign currency, overdraft/building/market/convertible instruments, payment-in-kind, covenant modification, refinancing/extinguishment, disputed balance, missing allocation, or any effective-interest/fee treatment that the frozen pattern cannot calculate. An ordinary variable rate is supported only when the lender statement supplies the actual accrued amount without Talli making a market-value or legal judgment; this is the narrower [#172 normal-bank-loan boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

### Owner-to-company loan

Support only a NOK loan where a Norwegian personal or corporate owner lends to the company. Require a signed agreement, direction of funding, principal, dates, ordinary interest and repayment terms, bank evidence, related-party identity, and documented corporate approvals or an evidenced exemption. Related-party agreements can require board approval, a declaration, and filing depending on amount and facts under [aksjeloven § 3-8](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A73-8), and related-party income or assets may be adjusted to arm's-length conditions under [skatteloven § 13-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A713-1).

Post funding as debit `bank` / credit `debt to owner`; post documented interest as debit `interest expense` / credit `interest payable or bank`; split repayments between principal and interest from the agreement/statement. Talli records the executed, evidenced transaction and does not decide whether terms are commercially ordinary.

Hard block company-to-personal direction, missing or backdated agreement, unclear beneficial lender, unusual/subordinated/convertible/profit-linked/payment-in-kind terms, undocumented waiver, foreign party/currency, or unresolved approval/arm's-length facts. Company credit or security to a shareholder is regulated by [aksjeloven § 8-7](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A78-7), and a company/group loan or security to a personal shareholder is generally treated as dividend for tax under [skatteloven § 10-11 fourth paragraph](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-11); it is expressly outside the [#172 product boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

### Norwegian intercompany loan

Require two identified Norwegian companies, direction, group/related-party relationship, signed NOK agreement, principal, dates, ordinary interest and repayment terms, written material agreement evidence, approvals, lender statements, and bank matches. Intra-group transactions must rest on ordinary commercial terms and principles, and material agreements must be written, under [aksjeloven § 3-9](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A73-9); the arm's-length tax rule is [skatteloven § 13-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A713-1).

Post the actual lender's receivable and borrower's debt symmetrically when both entities are in Talli, with one shared event identity and independently balanced journals. Post interest to the separately identified group-interest categories shown in [regnskapsloven § 6-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A76-1). A one-sided ledger may post only from complete counterparty evidence and must retain the counterparty reference.

For Talli's account and SAF-T mapping, parent-to-subsidiary loan receivables use `1320`, other same-group loan receivables use `1325`, and group-company liabilities use `2260`, matching the [Skatteetaten-published SAF-T standard-account list](https://github.com/Skatteetaten/saf-t/blob/master/General%20Ledger%20Standard%20Accounts/CSV/General_Ledger_Standard_Accounts_4_character_2020.csv). These stay distinct from owner receivables (`1370`) and owner liabilities (`2255`).

Hard block foreign party/currency, circular/netted/unmatched transfers, cash pooling, subordination, convertibility, capitalization/waiver, non-arm's-length or undocumented terms, inconsistent counterparties, or any [§ 6-41 interest-limitation](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A76-41) exposure that the applicable tax module cannot resolve. This implements the [#172 “normal Norwegian intercompany loan” boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

### Dividend received by the holding company

Require payer identity and Norwegian residence, share count/class and ownership period, final dividend decision, entitlement date, amount, payment evidence, legality evidence, and the tax-engine result for exemption-method treatment. Under [skatteloven § 2-38](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A72-38), qualifying corporate share income is generally exempt but three percent of qualifying dividends is generally income; the three-percent inclusion does not apply to qualifying same-group dividends under its sixth paragraph. Skatteetaten's current source-owner summary confirms the general three-percent treatment and enumerates important exclusions ([Skatteetaten: exemption method](https://www.skatteetaten.no/bedrift-og-organisasjon/skatt/skattemelding-naringsdrivende/fradrag/aksjer/fritaksmetoden/)).

Talli freezes the permitted NRS 8 policy of recognizing ordinary received dividend when the final dividend decision is made: debit `dividend receivable` / credit `dividend income`, then debit bank / credit receivable on payment. [NRS 8 section 5.3.2](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf) permits decision-year recognition and requires a material return of invested capital to reduce the investment instead of being income.

Hard block foreign payer/shareholder facts, multiple or unusual share classes/rights, unlawful or disputed dividend, dividend close to acquisition or other facts that make return-of-capital classification material, missing decision, unclear exemption-method eligibility, withholding/foreign tax credit, or a different previously frozen recognition policy. Those cases need judgment and fall outside the [#172 ordinary-dividend route](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

### Dividend to the holding company's owner

Require one ordinary share class, shareholder register and per-share entitlement, board proposal, general-meeting decision or valid board authorization, source annual accounts or interim balance where applicable, available-distribution calculation, adequate-equity/liquidity confirmation, amount, entitlement/payment dates, and bank evidence. The distribution ceiling is in [aksjeloven § 8-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A78-1), and the continuing equity/liquidity condition is in [§ 3-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A73-4). The general meeting decides on the board's proposal, subject to the statutory routes for ordinary and extraordinary dividends, under [§ 8-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A78-2) and [§ 8-2 a](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A78-2a).

For an ordinary year-end dividend, follow the frozen small-entity policy in [NRS 8 section 5.3.1](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf): debit distributable equity and credit `dividend payable` for the evidenced proposal; settle debit payable / credit bank after valid approval and payment. Additional or extraordinary dividend is recognized as short-term debt when decided, as described in NRS 8 sections 5.3.3 and 6.2.1.7.1. Talli must separately retain proposal, approval, registration/interim-balance, and payment states; a proposal alone must never authorize payment.

Hard block multiple/unusual classes or rights, insufficient or uncertain distributable capacity, absent decision/authorization, an unregistered or stale interim-balance route, non-pro-rata allocation, foreign shareholder tax/withholding, disputed entitlement, or any case where the board's adequate-equity/liquidity judgment has not been made and evidenced. Requiring that corporate judgment is not Talli making it.

### Straightforward group contribution

Require two qualifying Norwegian companies, exact ownership and voting chain at year-end, direction, amount, tax year, board/general-meeting evidence, available-distribution and adequate-equity/liquidity evidence, tax-engine allocation between deductible/taxable and non-deductible/non-taxable amounts, frozen cost-method accounting, and source/payment evidence. A deductible contribution is limited by the giver's otherwise taxable ordinary income under [skatteloven § 10-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-2); the recipient's same-year treatment is governed by [§ 10-3](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-3); and the Norwegian-company plus more-than-90-percent shares-and-votes conditions are in [§ 10-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-4). Company-law distribution limits apply under [aksjeloven § 8-5](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A78-5). Skatteetaten's current source-owner guidance confirms that both contributions with and without tax effect are reported ([Skatteetaten: group contributions](https://www.skatteetaten.no/bedrift-og-organisasjon/skatt/skattemelding-naringsdrivende/fradrag/utland-store-selskaper-og-konsern/konsernbidrag/)).

The only deterministic cost-method routes are those specified by [NRS 8 sections 5.4.1–5.4.3](https://www.regnskapsstiftelsen.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NRS-8-God-regnskapsskikk-for-sma-foretak-2025-desember.pdf):

| Direction | Deterministic accounting route |
| --- | --- |
| Subsidiary to parent | Parent recognizes the gross contribution as investment income only to the extent it is earned post-acquisition income; any material return of cost reduces the investment. Subsidiary posts the after-tax contribution directly against other equity. |
| Parent to subsidiary | Subsidiary records the after-tax contribution as other paid-in equity. Parent increases investment carrying value by the after-tax contribution, with an impairment check. Talli does not select the NRS 8 loss-cover simplification ad hoc. |
| Subsidiary to sister subsidiary | Giver records the after-tax contribution against other equity (first against other paid-in equity where NRS 8 requires); recipient records other paid-in equity. |

Other paid-in equity maps to `2035`; `2030` remains exclusively unregistered capital increase. The distinction follows the [Skatteetaten-published SAF-T standard-account list](https://github.com/Skatteetaten/saf-t/blob/master/General%20Ledger%20Standard%20Accounts/CSV/General_Ledger_Standard_Accounts_4_character_2020.csv) and prevents a group contribution from being misrepresented as an unregistered share issue.

Cash direction alone is therefore insufficient. Talli must not use one generic `received contribution income` or `given contribution expense` pair for every relationship; the relationship, tax effect, post-acquisition earnings/return-of-cost facts, and impairment facts select the route under the cited NRS 8 sections.

The gross tax amount, after-tax accounting amount, related tax, receivable/payable, and cash settlement must reconcile without netting away the audit trail. Hard block equity-method accounting, foreign/EEA extension, ownership or votes not proved at year-end, circular/multiple contributions, non-cash property, return-of-cost ambiguity, uncertain impairment, insufficient distribution capacity, inconsistent company-year, or any route outside the three frozen cases. The law permits broader cases; this is Talli's [#172 “straightforward” product boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

## Explicit fail-closed boundary

| Encountered fact | Why Talli must stop rather than judge |
| --- | --- |
| Payroll, VAT, customer invoicing, inventory, property, ongoing sales, audit-required entity, regulated activity, active trading, crypto, derivatives, complex reorganization, or a second accounting product/workaround | These are expressly outside the [owner-approved #172 boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104). The block is product eligibility, not a statement that the activity is unlawful. |
| Consolidated accounts required or applicability uncertain | A parent generally prepares consolidated accounts, with small-group and other statutory exceptions summarized by [Altinn's official group-accounts guidance](https://info.altinn.no/starte-og-drive/regnskap-og-revisjon/regnskap/arsregnskap/arsregnskap-for-konsern/). Talli supports only a company-year that does not require them under [#172](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104). |
| Foreign shareholder, foreign-currency account/transaction, foreign investment whose tax result is not already deterministic, foreign withholding or tax-credit question | Foreign-currency bookkeeping is not inherently unlawful—the regulation permits specified cases under [bokføringsforskriften § 4-2](https://lovdata.no/forskrift/2004-12-01-1558/%C2%A74-2)—and the exemption method contains fact-sensitive cross-border rules in [skatteloven § 2-38](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A72-38). Talli's NOK/Norwegian-only stop is the narrower [#172 boundary](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104). |
| Multiple share classes or unusual dividend rights | [Aksjeloven § 4-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A74-1) permits articles to create classes with different rights. Talli supports one ordinary class under [#172](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104), so it must not flatten differing rights into a pro-rata posting. |
| Company-to-personal loan or security | It invokes the distribution/credit limits in [aksjeloven § 8-7](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A78-7) and the personal-shareholder dividend treatment in [skatteloven § 10-11 fourth paragraph](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-11), and is expressly blocked by [#172](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104). |
| Repayment or other distribution of paid-in share capital, including a capital reduction with cash/assets to owners | It is a different company-law and tax route from loss coverage under [aksjeloven § 12-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-1) and [§ 12-6](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A712-6), [skatteloven § 10-11 second paragraph](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-11), and [§ 10-35](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A710-35). It requires shareholder-specific paid-in-capital history and remains outside the [#172 deterministic route](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104), even if the legal procedure could be completed. |
| Material estimate, valuation, impairment, return-of-capital split, arm's-length term, distribution capacity, or tax classification not proved by frozen inputs/rules | The applicable accounting rule may require a best estimate under [regnskapsloven § 4-2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1998-07-17-56/%C2%A74-2), company-law judgment under [aksjeloven § 3-4](https://lovdata.no/lov/1997-06-13-44/%C2%A73-4), or related-party assessment under [skatteloven § 13-1](https://lovdata.no/lov/1999-03-26-14/%C2%A713-1). Talli must collect an authorized decision or block; it must not manufacture the judgment. |
| Volume, source quality, or an unrecognized pattern prevents full bookkeeping and all filings | “Ordinary volumes” is a [#172 product condition](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104), not a statutory number. The operational threshold is whether all sources can be processed, reconciled, retained, and filed correctly within product limits; failure means truthful stop, not sampled or partial completion. |

## Acceptance implications for implementation

For each supported pattern, automated tests must prove the balanced semantic entry, period, documentation requirements, idempotent retry, full reversal/replacement correction, locked-period behavior, bank and counterparty reconciliation, year-to-date roll-forward, applicable tax handoff, and stable generated reporting output. For every hard-block condition above, a negative test must prove that no economic posting, close, filing-ready state, or completion claim is created and that existing read/export data remains available. These are Talli controls derived from the completeness, audit-trail, and protection duties in [bokføringsloven § 4](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A74), correction duty in [§ 9](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A79), documentation duty in [§ 10](https://lovdata.no/lov/2004-11-19-73/%C2%A710), and the [#172 fail-closed product decision](https://github.com/kristianelmer/Talli/issues/172#issuecomment-5423455104).

Share/fund tests must additionally prove separate accounting and tax bases, share-class FIFO, fund acquisition/sale-year equity-share inputs, oversell rejection, and position/shareholder-register reconciliation. Direct owner-transfer tests must prove cap-table-only behavior, no company journal, annual-report/beneficial-owner status, buyer input-value lot continuity, and preservation of per-share paid-in capital. Cash-capital tests must prove decision/subscription/restricted-payment/confirmation/registration states, per-share nominal/premium/paid-in-capital attributes, and the exact reclassification from unregistered capital to nominal capital and premium. Loss-coverage reduction tests must prove no cash or owner value transfer, unchanged owners/share count and paid-in-capital history, unregistered-to-registered reclassification, minimum registered capital, and enforcement of the three-year dividend restriction. Repayment-of-paid-in-capital fixtures must always hard-block.

Temporal tests must select the legal/tax rule set by company-year and reject an unsupported version. The source register's access-date text is research evidence, not permission to apply 2026 rules to a different tax year.

## Known source and automation gaps

- No cited statute prescribes Talli's exact semantic accounts, source-ID schema, idempotency key, or journal API. Those remain product controls and must be tied to canonical generated contracts.
- The “join later, reconstruct from 1 January” behavior is a Talli completeness promise derived from the calendar-year and complete-bookkeeping requirements; no cited authority describes SaaS onboarding in those terms.
- Balanced double entry is the chosen Talli invariant. This document does not claim that the cited sections prescribe Talli's storage model or use that phrase verbatim.
- NRS 8 provides multiple permitted policies in places, notably received-dividend timing, prior-year corrections, deferred-tax assets, and some group-contribution treatments. Talli must freeze and disclose one supported choice per company-year; it cannot switch opportunistically.
- “Ordinary administration cost,” “ordinary loan terms,” adequate equity/liquidity, impairment, material return of capital, and some tax classifications can depend on facts or judgment. The sources do not supply a universal deterministic classifier. Missing proof is therefore a hard block, not a model inference.
- NRS 8 requires deferred-tax accounting for small taxable entities, subject to its limited deferred-tax-asset simplification. Complete implementation needs a versioned deterministic tax-difference engine or a hard block for any company-year it cannot calculate; posting only the tax payment is insufficient.
- NRS 8 permits accounting-policy choices for current investments, while the tax rules keep a separate basis and fund-equity calculation. Talli must version the frozen accounting policy, accounting lots, tax lots, market evidence, and provider-reported fund equity shares; the primary sources do not define the provider data contract.
- NRS 8 defines the unregistered/registered presentation for capital increases and reductions, but it does not prescribe Talli's event-state or API names. The implementation must nevertheless prevent a decision, subscription, or bank movement from being exposed as registered capital before Foretaksregisteret evidence.
- The legal text and NRS 8 define governing rules, not every SAF-T account mapping or filing-field mapping. Those mappings must be separately versioned and reconciled to the canonical ledger without changing the supported economic boundary.
