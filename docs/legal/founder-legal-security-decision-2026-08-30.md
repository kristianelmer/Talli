Kristian replied `Approved` to the following decision:

> I, Kristian Elmer, acting for ELMER WELFIS, organisation number 930 835 978, approve the following decisions for Talli:
>
> 1. Talli is a business-only holding-first accounting and filing app for simple Norwegian holding companies.
> 2. The customer company controls its company data, shareholder data, accounting documents, narrow ledger, and filing data. Talli uses that data only to provide and secure the service.
> 3. ELMER WELFIS controls the limited information needed for user accounts, access, security, support, optional website measurement, billing, and its own legal records.
> 4. Talli does not sell customer data. Talli does not use accounting documents, bank data, company data, or filing data for advertising.
> 5. Optional website measurement requires an active and separate yes. No thanks must be equally clear. Refusing must not reduce the service.
> 6. Website measurement uses a random session ID for no more than 30 minutes. Raw measurement events and the minimum proof of permission are kept for no more than 90 days. The short withdrawal record is kept for no more than 30 minutes.
> 7. Website measurement is not connected to an account, company, purchase, company-year, support case, refund, bank data, accounting data, documents, or filings. Talli calls this limited data pseudonymous, not anonymous.
> 8. Invited-pilot observation uses the normal Talli product. It adds only a limited observation record. It must not change any product result. Raw pilot observations are kept for no more than 90 days. The protected participant register is kept for 12 months after validation ends, unless an incident or legal requirement needs longer storage.
> 9. Full public launch requires invited-pilot observation to be off. All pilot permissions must be expired or removed.
> 10. Talli uses one NOK 1,490 company-year subscription. The price includes VAT only when VAT legally applies. The old monthly and filing-package price model is withdrawn.
> 11. Talli refunds a supported company-year when Talli accepts the case but fails because of Talli product logic or connection. Talli does not promise a refund for incorrect customer data, missing authority access, unsupported cases, external authority outages, or deadlines missed outside Talli control.
> 12. Before a customer leaves, Talli provides export and read-only access as already approved. Talli then returns or deletes customer-controlled data according to the customer choice and any legal duty that applies directly to Talli.
> 13. Talli does not use the customer own bookkeeping duty as a general reason to retain all customer data.
> 14. Talli does not appoint a formal privacy officer at launch. Talli is not a public authority and does not plan large-scale tracking or large-scale handling of sensitive personal information. This decision will be reviewed each year and after a major product or scale change.
> 15. Norwegian law governs the agreement. An authorised person must actively accept the exact current agreement for the named company. Passive use and pre-selected boxes do not count as acceptance.
> 16. Kristian Elmer is Talli security and incident owner before Talli becomes a separate company.
> 17. I accept that the current review used AI agents. It is not independent professional legal or security certification. I remain responsible for the final decisions.
> 18. I approve the code-level security design for the public, no-customer-data state. I do not approve real customer data until Talli has: identified exact Supabase and Vercel systems; tested hosted company separation and private document storage; limited support access to a specific case and time period; tested database and document restoration; verified logs, regions, external services, transfers, access, and deletion; verified automatic data cleanup; tested monitoring and incident handling; and recorded final legal-document versions and digital fingerprints.
> 19. I authorize the agents to correct the documents and product controls to match these decisions, run all no-cost checks, and prepare the final evidence. This does not authorize spending, paid upgrades, customer contact, real customer data, bank activation, production filing, advertising, or unrestricted public launch.
