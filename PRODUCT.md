# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Talli is for owners who manage one simple Norwegian holding AS themselves. They typically have limited accounting needs and return to the product around company events and the annual reporting cycle. Their job is to understand what must be done, provide the required information, review the result, and file correctly without navigating the surface area of a full accounting suite.

## Product Purpose

Talli makes annual reporting for a simple Norwegian holding company correct, seamless, and inexpensive. It provides the narrow ledger and guided workflows needed for the annual holding compliance loop while deliberately excluding payroll, invoicing, VAT, and other operating-company capabilities the primary user does not need.

Success means an owner can move from company data and holding actions to reviewed, filing-ready annual obligations with a clear sense of progress, cost, and responsibility.

## Positioning

Talli is the focused, owner-managed alternative to broad accounting suites for simple Norwegian holding companies: direct annual reporting without paying for operating-company complexity.

## Brand Personality

Talli is reassuring, plain-spoken, and meticulous. It should reduce anxiety without trivializing high-consequence work, explain accounting and authority concepts in ordinary owner language, and make correctness visible throughout the workflow.

The desired product experience is one of quiet confidence: smooth, systematic, intuitive, minimal, and easy to navigate.

## Anti-references

Talli should not visually imitate Fiken even though Fiken is a business and category reference. It should avoid generic AI-generated styling, decorative novelty, clutter, feature bloat, unexplained accounting jargon, and filing flows that feel intimidating or bureaucratic.

## Design Principles

1. **Make complexity feel bounded.** Turn the annual compliance loop into a finite sequence with an obvious current step, next action, and completion state.
2. **Speak to the owner first.** Use Norwegian-first, plain language and introduce accounting or authority terminology only when it improves understanding or accuracy.
3. **Make correctness visible.** Show validation, review status, assumptions, warnings, and filing readiness at the point where each matters.
4. **Earn trust through restraint.** Prefer familiar interaction patterns, calm hierarchy, and precise feedback over decoration or novelty.
5. **Protect the narrow promise.** Keep unsupported operating-company workflows out of the default experience and clearly escalate cases that no longer fit a simple holding AS.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Talli targets WCAG 2.2 AA. Core workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, screen readers, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, and reduced-motion preferences. No additional audience-specific accessibility requirements are currently identified.
