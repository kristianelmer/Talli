The minimum facts at the boundary are:

1. **Accepted and current company-year scope — Company Access.** Immutable company/year, admission and accepted/current assessment references; assessment/acceptance times; accepted/current answer digests; exact manifest version/hash and accepted promise/hash; legal acceptance identity/evidence. #208 must select the correct year's supported boundary and prove that year's rules, rather than copy 2026. Preserve accepted facts independently of later rechecks.
2. **Readiness and complete filing history — each filing owner, then Annual Compliance.** Each obligation attests its own readiness/hard blocks, actual production-submission history, terminal/unknown outcomes, corrections and coverage. A negative conclusion that no production filing occurred requires completeness across all three sources. An absent row, empty adapter or unknown outcome cannot establish that negative. #149 adds the aggregate versioned reference and current-validity verifier, without changing a filing owner's decision.
3. **Incident facts — their actual source owners.** Company Access attests accepted eligibility and later authoritative rechecks; billing's provider boundary attests recorded payment outcomes; filing owners attest filing failures/outcomes. Evidence must establish company/year, identity/version, event/effective/discovery timing and attribution. Billing alone classifies the facts under #177 and computes money. A Talli product/integration/capacity/deadline/filing-logic failure cannot be relabelled customer ineligibility.
4. **Original financial facts — Billing.** Original purchase/capture, first purchase, gross/refunded amounts, exact account/agreement/charge linkage, terms/consent and persisted refund/STOP records already belong to billing. They cannot establish filing history or incident cause. The existing refund receiver needs those financial facts joined to authoritative incident and submission evidence.
5. **Renewal prerequisites — their respective owners.** Company Access supplies supported target-year eligibility; Annual Compliance supplies independent charging readiness; Billing retains explicit recurring consent, cancellation, offer/price and recorded notice facts. A boolean policy input is not proof that its source exists.

The current receiver contracts expose the intended seam without implementing the missing producers:

- `AnnualCheckoutPrerequisites` contains an accepted-basis reference, readiness reference/digest, evaluation time and ready flag. The persistence adapter verifies Company Access's owned purchase basis under locks, requires the exact readiness verifier to return true, limits evaluation age to five minutes, and rechecks the purchase basis after the verifier await. A synthetic digest is insufficient.
- `AnnualRefundFacts` carries reason, purchase/first-purchase/acceptance/discovery/effective/block/submission times, year, amounts and evidence reference. The new-claim resolver currently returns no facts. The reason is billing's commercial classification; the type does not authorize a producer to decide liability.
- `AnnualRenewalFacts` and the deterministic renewal decision already name consent, cancellation, notices, target eligibility/readiness, price and dates. They do not constitute the final durable automatic renewal runtime.
- Existing annual workflows declare billing's public package only. Real source bindings must be declared through the approved public-contract/workflow mechanism; no private cross-capability table reads or hidden dependency cycle.

Unresolved facts to carry into the source handoff:

- Exact producer query/event schemas, source identifiers, completeness/version semantics and verifier signatures are not yet published by the missing filing/Annual Compliance modules. Their issue obligations define the required facts, not a completed interface.
- Validated 2027 or any other new-year support is not established. #208 must supply that year's source/rule evidence; any changed approved boundary reopens #172.
- Any required non-payment incident outside the already identified owners still needs an exact canonical producer and serialized placement before final #192. The approved amendment creates no generic incident service.
- Final #192 still owns actual source binding, ordinary paid entitlement, durable automatic renewal/refund processing with authorized service authority, and source-backed concurrency/replay/rollback proof. Existing observation/recovery worker authority does not authorize new charges or refund decisions, and must not be expanded using fabricated owner claims.
- All original #192 acceptance remains: actual designated MT including the outstanding original recurring capture/refund/STOP, representative customer/operator and browser/mobile flows, security/privacy/accounting, independent exact-revision review, two linked complete immutable gates, protected integration and exact-main Release/Preview. These are separate from merely publishing source contracts. No assertion about their future completion is made here.
