The refund adapter currently compares evidence reference/year, capture and first-purchase times, acceptance, gross/refunded amounts, and rejects future discovery/submission dates. Source company is therefore bound through the command/resolver; `AnnualRefundFacts` itself has no company field, source-version field, coverage record, incident ID or correction graph. Do not mistake the flat receiver's successful construction for proof of those missing producer obligations.

Critically, `modules/billing/annual_policy.py:76–88` uses `production_submission_at is None` as “no submission” for its first-30-days full-refund branch. Approved scope therefore requires authoritative completeness across RF, tax and accounts before final integration can supply that absence. An empty result from RF alone, a missing adapter, an unresolved effect or a latest-row lookup cannot establish it. Exact multi-source scope and reduction into this existing timestamp receiver remain to be resolved by the responsible owners; this note proposes no algorithm or new schema.

Live acquisition stays unavailable: `application/annual_checkout_prerequisites.py:1–15` rejects the missing authoritative source; `application/annual_billing.py:233–239` uses it when no collector is supplied; `adapters/supabase_annual_billing.py:193` constructs checkout with the unavailable default verifier. Refund resolution likewise defaults to `_unavailable_source` (`adapters/postgres_annual_refund.py:79–88`). Fixtures do not replace these facts.

## Missing source obligations and unresolved facts

1. **RF readiness:** publish immutable RF-owned readiness and non-billing hard blocks with exact company/year/obligation, evidence identity/version/time and currentness. Preserve existing supported-case/rule/schema semantics, accepted warnings and review facts. RF cannot assert the other two obligations or redefine Company Access eligibility. The final source identity, version shape and currentness/invalidation contract are not settled by existing Billing strings.
2. **Complete actual history:** publish RF production submissions and their terminal/unknown progression with attributable evidence and explicit coverage. Preserve prior/superseded attempts and receipts. Distinguish an unattempted record from an attempted unknown outcome, and actual production from simulation/test evidence. Existing local lists lack an authoritative completeness boundary; no missing-row default is admissible proof.
3. **Corrections:** retain observable supersession/correction facts and their source evidence. Do not erase earlier production activity when a record is superseded. The approved handoff does not itself authorize a new correction writer, broader RF case or unsupported production capability.
4. **RF incidents:** attest only RF-owned failure/outcome facts, including exact company/year, evidence identity, source version, event/discovery timing and attributable cause where evidence supports it. Billing's `AnnualRefundReason` values—change of mind, Talli acceptance/delivery failure, new unsupported condition, customer unresolved—are commercial classifications, not RF incident labels to manufacture. Missing attribution remains unavailable; identify any required incident with no canonical producer rather than invent a generic owner.
5. **Aggregation/final binding:** #149 must combine all three public sources without writing obligation state, retain completeness boundaries, return versioned scoped identity and verify current validity. #208 separately supplies validated target-year admission. Final #192 joins these to Billing-owned financial facts and separate owner/support/worker authority; RF must not mutate Billing or fabricate an owner session.

This is a requirements inventory, not a proposed contract design. Exact RF public API names, payload schemas, completeness boundaries, incident taxonomy and invalidation semantics remain source-stage work subject to #151's entry and exit gates. Neither this inventory nor an immutable DTO alone establishes source authority or final #192 readiness.
