**Do not certify both historical aliases under the existing whole-resource exception.** Removing the two `public.opening_*` physical entries is correct after final-contract drop is actually proved. Assigning `table:opening_shareholders` to its RF-owned successor is consistent with its full semantic ownership. The `table:opening_balance_setups` alias has a different implication.

`architecture/database-catalog.schema.json` permits `compatibilityResources` only on capability-business entries, with one capability owner. `scripts/check-architecture.mjs:1473–1497` builds a single `resourceOwners` map from those aliases; line2800 supplies that map as `resourceOwner` to the compatibility registry deletion check. It is an ownership assertion used for authorization, not merely a neutral historical rename or lookup hint. It cannot express a split across RF and Ledger.

ADR0013:83–94 requires the database catalog to assign the exact retired future scope's resource to the active capability, preserving every retained future-owned resource and occurrence. The #151 approved inventory explicitly assigns only identity/share facts from `opening_balance_setups` to RF, with `bank_balance` remaining Ledger-owned (`storage-ownership-inventory.md`, Opening data section). The original #157 archive GET selected bank_balance along with those share/identity fields. The new joined read honestly obtains them from two owners. The old physical relation disappearing does not change that source split.

Consequently, mapping the old whole-table resource token to RF alone would let the checker certify a broader single-owner claim than the implementation/spec supports. Prose saying that RF does not own bank amounts cannot narrow that machine claim. Mapping the token to Ledger instead is equally inaccurate for the share fields. Declaring it on both is rejected as ambiguous and is not an approved representation.

Include the one original `compat-company-archive-persistence` / `table:opening_balance_setups` / `GET` scope at `apps/web/app/archive/[companyId]/[incomeYear]/download/route.ts` as an explicit split-resource retirement in the pending finite amendment, unless a prior exact owner decision already covers that tuple.
